### 🏕️ Create a group
Give your group a name (like "Midnight H2" or "Goa Trip"), add your name, and invite your friends.

### 📸 Scan a receipt
Tap **Scan receipt**. Just snap a photo or pick one from your camera roll, and give it a few seconds to read the text.

### 🧐 Check the items
Quickly check the list. If anything is highlighted in yellow, just tap to fix it. Make sure the final total matches your physical bill.

### 🍕 Who had what
Tap your friends' faces to tag who bought what. If you all shared a pizza, just hit **Everyone**. If an item was bought more than once (like Maggi × 3), you will see options like **Split into 3** to easily divide the quantities.

### 💸 Add an expense without a receipt
No receipt? Tap **Add manually**. You can split the bill equally, type in exact amounts, or use percentages. You can also tap the new **category chips** to easily organize and label your expenses.

### 🤝 Settle up
When it's time to pay, the app automatically does the math so your group makes as few transfers as possible. You can settle balances instantly using the **Pay with UPI** button. Once someone pays you back, just tap **Mark paid**.

---

### 💡 A few quick tips:
- **Perfect scans:** Put your receipt flat on a dark table in good light so there are no shadows.
- **Dark mode:** You can switch between light and dark themes using the icon on the top right of the home screen.
- **Avatars:** Tap your profile icon to pick a new avatar.
